About Chahat

I'm a Spanish and English trainer at Inlingua, helping learners who want to go beyond passing a test and start speaking with confidence.

“You don't learn swimming by reading about water.”

My path into languages started at the Escuela de Español, where I earned my Diploma de Español while working in sales — learning to pitch, connect, and close in a new language from day one. That foundation shaped how I teach today: practical, conversational, and rooted in real situations.

I've also managed international client communications in Spanish across LATAM markets at enterprise scale — so whether you're preparing for exams, interviews, travel, or professional conversations, I bring lived experience to every session.
